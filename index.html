What is expected

  • Describe only what is clearly visible in the short video.
  • Do not infer intent, emotions, or background story.
  • Focus on objects, actions, positions, and visible details.
  • Each scene lasts 60 seconds.
  • Keep responses to 1-2 factual lines (max 400 characters).

How scoring works

  • Responses are evaluated using automated checks and trainer review.
  • Scoring focuses on accuracy, relevance, and clarity.
  • There are no trick questions.
  • Creativity is not required.
  • Partial but accurate observations are acceptable.

This exercise measures observation skills, not writing style.
